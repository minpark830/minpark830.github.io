Crazyflie swarm controller

Sep 2024 – Jun 2025

A framework for flying Crazyflie micro-drones in formation. Leader and follower apps written in C run on the drones' firmware, while a FastAPI server relays commands and position estimates over Crazyradio links. Followers hold triangle and line formations relative to the leader, localizing with an extended Kalman filter on optical-flow and range data. My Cal Poly senior project in the CPSquare lab with Nikolas Tambornini, flight-tested with three drones. I wrote the leader's firmware app and the FastAPI server.

C on the Crazyflie firmware (FreeRTOS), Python, FastAPI, WebSockets, Crazyradio 2.0, Flow deck
